About Me

Transforming Transportation Through Technology and Innovation.

I am an Intelligent Transportation Systems (ITS) and Traffic Engineer at AtkinsRéalis with experience supporting major transportation initiatives for NJDOT, NJTA, NJTPA, NYSDOT, NYSTA, MDOT, and MTA. My work focuses on transportation technology deployment, fiber-optic communications, roadway safety, traffic operations, and intelligent infrastructure design.

I am passionate about leveraging engineering, research, and technology to improve transportation system performance and safety. My interests span connected and emerging vehicle technologies, transportation operations, safety analytics, fiber communications, and data-driven decision-making that helps agencies deliver more reliable and efficient transportation networks.
